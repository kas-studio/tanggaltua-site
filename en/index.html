Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Tanggal Tua" mean?

In Indonesia, "Tanggal Tua" (literally "old dates") is the beloved everyday phrase for the final 10–11 days of the month when salaries have run dry and everyone gets creative with rice-cooker meals until payday on the 1st.

Is Tanggal Tua truly free and playable 100% offline?

Yes! All 8 Kasta difficulty tiers, 36 secret recipes, 8 hometown trinkets, and 12 permanent Room 04 upgrades (including Oyen the ginger cat) can be unlocked purely through gameplay savings. Everything runs locally on your phone with an authentic Bahasa Indonesia story with English HUD/glossary support, zero internet connection required, and zero forced ads.

What is the 3-Day Daily Challenge (#TTH) & Survival Receipt?

Every day deals an identical 72-hour survival seed (Days 28–30) to all players worldwide. At the end of any run, the game prints a shareable retro convenience-store Thermal Receipt with a playable rival score link!

Who made Tanggal Tua?

Tanggal Tua is crafted by KAS Studio, an independent game and multimedia studio in South Jakarta, Indonesia. Check out our Press & Creator Kit for full fact sheets and high-resolution media assets.
