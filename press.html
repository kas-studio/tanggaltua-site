Deskripsi Siap Kutip (Bahasa Indonesia)

Ringkasan 1 Paragraf

Tanggal Tua adalah game strategi dan simulasi bertahan hidup anak kos buatan studio independen Jakarta Selatan, KAS Studio. Dimulai pada Tanggal 20 setelah mengirim uang gaji ke orang tua di kampung, pemain harus bertahan 11 hari menuju Tanggal 1 dengan sisa uang pas-pasan di dompet: memilih 1 dari 8 Jimat Anak Kos, berburu 16 bahan diskon sore di Gang Kancil, menyusun puzzle kulkas bersama ukuran 5×4 di sela kotak makan tetangga kos, meracik 36 resep di dalam satu panci Magic Com 450VA tanpa membuat listrik jeklek, mengejar 3 kerja sampingan akhir pekan (Kurir Ojol, Lapak CFD & Studio TV), hingga menolak godaan spam pinjol di tengah malam.

Boilerplate Description (English)

One-Paragraph Summary

Tanggal Tua is a cozy, 100% offline single-player survival and cooking strategy roguelite created by South Jakarta indie developer KAS Studio, playable in full English and Bahasa Indonesia. Named after Indonesia's everyday slang for the broke final stretch of the month, the game drops players into a bustling Jakarta boarding house on the 20th with only spare change left in their wallet after sending money home to family. Across 11 days until payday, players draft 1 of 8 roguelite hometown trinkets, fit 16 polyomino groceries into a shared 5×4 dorm fridge, cook 36 secret dishes inside a single 450VA electric rice cooker while flicking off room appliances to avoid tripping the circuit breaker, run 3 weekend side hustles, and share retro Struk 2.0 convenience-store Survival Receipts with friends.

About KAS Studio

KAS Studio (kasstudio.id) is an independent game studio based in South Jakarta, Indonesia, crafting tactile, culturally grounded cross-platform games including Tanggal Tua (tanggaltua.id), Pung (pungmahjong.com), and AkaSphere (akasphere.co).
